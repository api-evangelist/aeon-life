# Aeon Life

> Aeon Life Insurance Company, Ltd. (百年人寿保险股份有限公司, "AEON LIFE") is a Chinese life
> insurer headquartered in Dalian, Liaoning, established on 1 June 2009 with registered capital
> of RMB 7.9048 billion. It writes life, health and personal accident insurance and other
> personal-insurance lines, reinsurance of those lines, and regulated insurance-fund investment
> business, across twenty provinces and municipalities in China. Customer service and complaints
> run through the national hotline 95542.
